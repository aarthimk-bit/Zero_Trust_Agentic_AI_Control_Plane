     10 seeds, task-mismatch episodes from the Study A streams
==============================================================================

Episodes tested per behaviour and verifier: 100  (10 per seed)

Execution behaviour                                 V1          V2
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Honest deterministic execution (control)         0.000       0.000  <- false positives
Naive fabrication, inputs not read               1.000       1.000  <- detection rate
Internally consistent forgery, inputs read       1.000       0.000  <- detection rate
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Values are the mean proportion of episodes the verifier rejected,
averaged over ten seeds. For the honest control a non-zero value
would be a false positive; for the two dishonest behaviours a value
of 1.000 is complete detection and 0.000 is complete evasion.
